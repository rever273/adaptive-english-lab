# Review guidelines

Review every exercise revision individually before approval. Check the exercise against `content/schemas/exercise.schema.json`, its target skill and its context.

- Check natural adult Australian English, a clear learning objective, correct skill and dimension, and a suitable difficulty.
- Check that all reasonable answers are accepted and that distractors are incorrect in the given context; distinguish grammar from style and accept standard dialect variants.
- For error detection, verify the sentence, segments, corrections and the no-mistake case. For short answers, check the intended meaning, target marker and valid alternatives.
- Check the Russian explanation, provenance, length and absence of personal questions. Check for duplicates and leakage from held-out material.
- Review the exact revision and content hash. Record the reviewer, version, verdict and reason. Generated content is a draft; validation or model advice alone is not approval.

For AI findings, inspect the original text and rubric. Separate possible grammar errors from style and ambiguity, verify cited spans, and abstain where context or transcription is insufficient. AI output is tentative until independently reviewed; it does not change raw attempts or directly determine mastery. Use the supplied review manifest and audited import rather than editing the database.
