# Vocabulary replenishment

Follow [common.md](common.md). Only planned words from the visible personal dictionary may be targets; dictionary translations and CEFR are tentative context, not authoritative. Do not overwrite dictionary entries. Preserve target lemma and evidence family; choose the correct sense, part of speech and surface form in a new adult everyday context.

Recognition: reconstruct every option in the gap; options need parallel grammatical forms, plausible meanings and one defensible key. Recall: use a controlled clue with meaning/POS/first letter, no answer leakage; list all acceptable forms/standard spellings and keep 1–5 word answers consistent in payload and vocabulary metadata. Collocation: verify the natural pattern and exclude alternative valid partners in the supplied context. Check option rationaleRu, sentenceRu, wordRu and explanation. Stylistically less frequent is not ungrammatical. Check noun/verb polysemy and irregular inflections manually; never use naïve suffix generation as approval.

All metadata are part of the reviewed hash. Unknown alternatives remain uncertain; fix the context or reject. Retain the shared no-repeat mechanism, family separation and separation from Grammar Mastery. No cards/SRS, audio, cloud services or automatic approval.
